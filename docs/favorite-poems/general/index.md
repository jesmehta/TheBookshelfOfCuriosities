# Favourite Poems

Two of these (*Kubla Khan*, *Ozymandias*) were also read in the [British Poetry workshop](../workshop/index.md); *Ulysses* and *The Waste Land* have a workshop counterpart too, in a shorter or plainer form than the versions here.

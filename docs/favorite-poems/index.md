# Favourite Poetry

- **[Favourite Poems](general/index.md)**  
My favourite poems, which I keep collecting, printing, pasting in notebooks and on cupboard doors, to serve reminders, warnings, nostalgia, hope and inspiration.  

<br>

- **[British Poetry Workshop (May 2021)](workshop/index.md)**  
In the summer of 2021, while we all stayed put in our homes as far as we could, and online workshops thrived, Instucen (India Study Center) put together a 3 week long British Poetry workshop, discussing the British canon, from Chaucer to Eliot. These are the poems we read, and a few more I found along the way.

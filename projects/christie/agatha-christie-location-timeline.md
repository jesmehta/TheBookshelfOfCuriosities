# Agatha Christie — Location & Timeline Reference Table

Confidence key: **High** = author-confirmed (dedication, autobiography, in-text naming, or documented map endpapers) | **Moderate** = strong internal textual clues + established fan/scholar consensus (e.g. John Curran's notebook research) | **Low** = naming-pattern inference or single weak clue, no direct confirmation.

In-universe time defaults to "contemporary with publication" for a book unless Christie gives an explicit clue (season, historical setting, wartime reference) — these are flagged.

**Notes for the data model:**
- Christie's in-universe chronology is notoriously inconsistent for long-running characters — Poirot's stated retirement age in *Styles* (1920) makes his continued active detection through *Curtain* (set ~1940s) a well-known continuity problem fans call "the Poirot age paradox." Worth a boolean flag (`chronology_flag`) in your schema rather than trying to force a clean birth-year field.
- "Confidence" here reflects location-attribution confidence, not publication-date confidence — publication dates themselves are all high-confidence bibliographic fact.
- Several entries (marked Low) genuinely have no scholarly consensus; I'd recommend a `speculative` boolean rather than silently treating my inference here as fact in your Bookshelf dataset.

# Agatha Christie — Location & Timeline Reference Table (v2)

**Confidence key**: High = author-confirmed (dedication, autobiography, in-text naming, documented map endpapers) | Moderate = strong textual clues + scholarly/fan consensus | Low = naming-pattern inference or single weak clue, no direct confirmation.

**Note on scope**: Novels, T&T, and the two character-driven collections (Quin, Parker Pyne) are broken out to story level below. The remaining minor anthologies (Listerdale Mystery, Golden Ball, Double Sin, Poirot's Early Cases, Miss Marple's Final Cases, While the Light Lasts) are still grouped — flagged in Notes as candidates for a follow-up granular pass if useful.

**Schema suggestions carried over from the previous pass:**
- `chronology_flag` (boolean) — for Poirot's age-paradox books, esp. *Curtain*.
- `speculative` (boolean) — for every Low-confidence location row above.
- `real_location_type` (enum: named-directly / house-model / naming-pattern / travel-biographical / unconfirmed) — the "Reasoning" column above naturally sorts into these five buckets, which might be a cleaner filter than free text for your editor.
- `recycled_plot` (boolean + linked title) — flagging cases like "The Regatta Mystery" and "Death on the Nile" (short story vs. novel) where Christie reused a plot/title across different sleuths or formats, since your dataset will otherwise show two unrelated near-duplicate titles.
